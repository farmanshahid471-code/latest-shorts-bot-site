Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Latest Shorts Bot handles information when a user connects a TikTok account and uses the application's publishing features.

1. Information processed

The application may process:

  • TikTok open ID and basic profile information.
  • TikTok display name.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Videos selected or generated for publishing.
  • Video titles, descriptions, hashtags, and privacy selections.
  • Publishing status, error information, and TikTok post identifiers.

2. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Verify the TikTok account selected by the user.
  • Upload and publish videos requested by the user.
  • Renew expired OAuth access tokens.
  • Display publishing status and prevent unintended duplicate posts.
  • Diagnose failed authorization or publishing requests.

3. TikTok passwords

The application does not request, receive, or store TikTok passwords. TikTok authorization is completed through TikTok's official OAuth service.

4. Storage

OAuth credentials and publishing records are stored in the user's private local application configuration. Users are responsible for protecting the computer or server on which the application is installed.

5. Sharing

Information is transmitted to TikTok when necessary to verify an account, upload a video, publish content, renew authorization, or retrieve publishing status. Personal information is not sold.

6. Retention

Account credentials and publishing records are retained while the account remains configured in the application. Users may remove an account from the application to delete its locally stored configuration and publishing records.

7. Revoking access and deletion

Users may disable TikTok publishing in the application, remove the connected account, or revoke the application's access through their TikTok account settings. Deletion requests can also be submitted using the contact address below.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored credentials. However, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should not disclose access tokens, refresh tokens, or application secrets.

9. Children's privacy

The application is not directed to children and is intended only for users permitted to manage and publish content under applicable platform rules.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices or legal obligations change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.

11. Contact
